Where you are

Between the lake
and the Atlantic

All Points East is a small, tranquil campground at the far eastern end of Prince Edward Island — North Lake on one side, the ocean on the other. Forty sites, mown green space, spruce for shade, and the kind of dark sky you only get when the nearest town is a drive away.

We're family owned and operated, and proud to call eastern PEI home. Some guests roll in for a night on their way around the Island. Others book the same site every summer and treat the place like a second address. Both are welcome, and both get the same greeting at the office door.

Days here are simple: coffee on the step, tuna boats heading out of North Lake harbour, a beach walk before supper, and a fire once the wind drops.

Big rig or two-person tent, the welcome is the same. Washrooms and hot showers are open all season and included with every site.

Full service

  • 30 or 50 amp power

Power & water

Two-way sites with power and water — same price as full service, so choose the spot you like best.

  • 15, 30 or 50 amp power
  • Water hookup
  • Dump station on site
  • Fire ring · picnic table

Tent sites

Unserviced pads among the trees for tents and small trailers — quieter corners, softer ground.

  • Water nearby
  • Shaded & sheltered
  • Showers included
  • Fire ring · picnic table

Our one rule

Camp with kindness

To your neighbours

Sites here are close enough to say good morning across. Keep the music low after dark, wave when you drive the loop, and lend a hand backing in.

To the shore

Pack out what you bring in, keep fires in the ring, and leave the dune grass where it grows. This coast stays beautiful because campers look after it.

To yourself

There's no schedule to keep here. Sleep in, read the afternoon away, and let the ocean set the pace for a few days.

When are you open?

The campground runs from late April through early November. The in-ground pool opens late June and stays open to mid-September, weather permitting.

How do I book a site?

Call us at (902) 327-0356 or send an email to camp@allpointseastcampground.ca with your dates, the size of your rig and the hookups you need. You can also use the request form below and we'll get back to you.

Will my rig fit?

We have 15, 30 and 50 amp service and pull-through sites for larger rigs. Tell us your length when you call and we'll put you on a site that suits.

Are showers included?

Yes. Washroom facilities and hot showers are open all season and included with your camping fee — no coins, no tokens.

Is there a dump station?

There is. Full-service sites have sewer at the pad, and everyone else can use the on-site dump station on the way out.

What's nearby for supplies?

Souris has groceries, fuel, a hospital and walk-in clinics, fresh seafood, banks and restaurants. The office keeps firewood, ice cream and a few essentials on hand.
