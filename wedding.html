The basics

Which sections you want

Switch off anything you are not using and it disappears from the tabs. Nothing is deleted — turn it back on and your data is still there.

Destination wedding mode

Turn this on for a wedding away from home. It adds the travel-and-paperwork tasks a local wedding never needs — passports, guest travel logistics, shipping décor, and the marriage licence rules of wherever you're marrying — and shows a reference panel on the Checklist tab.

Your data

Everything is saved in this browser only — nothing is uploaded anywhere. Export a backup file if you want to move to another computer, or share the plan with someone else.
