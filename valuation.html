Investor Capital to Repay

Capital that must be repaid out of the sale, ranking behind the mortgage and ahead of you. Enter each obligation once; the return accrues to whatever closing date is being tested, so the Sale Price tab and the exit below both deduct the right amount for their own date. Accrual is monthly prorated — whole months from the start month plus the day fraction of the closing month — which is the basis the buyout workbook uses, so these figures tie to the redemption schedule rather than approximating it.

Retired from the refinance, not from the sale. The thirteen Maison Maritime House preferred shareholders — $1,000,000 of principal at 5%, interest paid to 31 August 2025 and running from 1 September 2025 — are being paid in full out of the $5,000,000 Equitable facility advanced 22 July 2026. Francistheriault Ventures lends MMH the money, MMH redeems the preferred shares, and MNP has it structured as a redemption at principal with a non-eligible dividend for the accrued difference. They are therefore set to rides through and are carried here for the record only: deducting them from a Hollis sale as well would double-count the whole $1,000,000. What I Need From You —

Tax on a corporate disposition splits into two entirely separate charges that behave differently: recapture of capital cost allowance, which is fully taxable and depends on the undepreciated balance you have been depreciating against, and a capital gain, of which half is taxable and half credits your capital dividend account and comes out tax free. Neither can be estimated from the sale price alone — they need the cost base and the CCA history for each parcel.
